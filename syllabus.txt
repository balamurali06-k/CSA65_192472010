COURSE: Natural Language Processing

UNIT 1: INTRODUCTION TO NLP
Topics include:
- Introduction to Natural Language Processing
- Applications of NLP
- Text preprocessing
- Tokenization
- Stemming
- Lemmatization
- Regular expressions
- N-gram language models

UNIT 2: LANGUAGE MODELS
Topics include:
- Unigram models
- Bigram models
- Trigram models
- Probability estimation
- Smoothing techniques

UNIT 3: SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS
Topics include:
- Context Free Grammar
- Parse trees
- Constituency parsing
- Dependency parsing

UNIT 4: SEMANTIC ANALYSIS
Topics include:
- Word meaning
- Word embeddings
- Semantic similarity
- Named Entity Recognition
- Word Sense Disambiguation

UNIT 5: TRANSFORMERS AND LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS
Topics include:
- Attention mechanism
- Transformer architecture
- BERT
- GPT
- Text generation
- Question answering
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
